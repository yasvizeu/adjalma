Today we are reviewing Past Simple, did/didn't, do/does and interpretation with a Scrum Master daily meeting dialogue.

Dialogue

Scrum Daily Meeting

Lucas: Good morning, everyone. Let’s start the daily meeting.
Marina: Yesterday I finished the login system and fixed two bugs.
Lucas: Great. Did you test everything?
Marina: Yes, I did. Everything worked well.
Lucas: Nice. What are you working on today?
Marina: Today I’m going to develop the dashboard page.
Lucas: Perfect. Adjalma, what about you?
Adjalma: Yesterday I talked to the client and updated the project documentation.
Lucas: Did the client like the new features?
Adjalma: Yes, he did. He said the platform looked much better now.
Lucas: Excellent. Do you have any blockers?
Adjalma: No, I don’t. But I need the final API documentation.
Lucas: Okay. I’ll send it after the meeting.
